In short

MyPowerfulNotes keeps your notes on your own devices, encrypted. We don't run a server, you don't make an account with us, and the app has no advertising, analytics or crash reporting. We can't see your notes, and we don't collect information about you.

This policy explains what the app does with your information, including the few times it connects to other services, and only when you ask it to.

Who we are

MyPowerfulNotes ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent app for Windows and Android, developed in Australia. This policy covers the apps and this website.

To contact us about privacy, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so don't include personal details or anything from your notes; say you'd like to talk privately and we'll arrange a private way to reply.

Your notes stay on your device

Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, typed text, drawings, imported PDFs, saved web pages and settings are stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your device: Windows Data Protection on a PC, and the Android Keystore (with your fingerprint, face or PIN) on Android.

When you start, the app gives you a recovery code. It's the only other way to open your notes, for example on a new device. Only you hold it: it's never sent to us, and we can't reset it or recover your notes without it.

The app doesn't send your notes, or anything about how you use it, to us or anyone else.

Sync with your own OneDrive or Google Drive (optional)

If you turn on sync, you sign in to your own Microsoft or Google account and the app keeps copies of its files there. Files are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded, so Microsoft and Google store only data they can't read. We never receive your files. The sign-in is kept only on your device.

  • OneDrive: the app can only use its own folder under Apps in your OneDrive (permission Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder), plus openid, profile and offline_access to stay signed in and show which account is connected. It can't see your other files.
  • Google Drive: the app asks to see, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata): a hidden folder only this app can use. It also asks for your email address and basic sign-in (openid, email) to show which account is connected. It can't see or change any other file in your Drive.

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide sync to you. It isn't sold, shared, used for advertising or read by people. MyPowerfulNotes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft and Google handle your account and storage under their own privacy policies, and may store data outside Australia. Because the files are encrypted before upload, your notes stay unreadable wherever they're stored.

Linking a phone or tablet by scanning a code on your PC shares the sync details between your own devices. The code is shown on your screen and isn't sent anywhere.

When the app uses other services

A few features rely on your device or on other companies. Each one only runs when you use it.

FeatureWhat happens
Reading handwritingOn Windows, the app uses Windows' own handwriting recognition on your PC. On Android, it uses Google ML Kit on your device: the first time you turn it on, a language model is downloaded from Google. Your ink is read on the device and isn't sent anywhere.
Talk to textUses your device's speech recognition (Windows voice typing, or Android's speech service). The app only receives the words. Microsoft or Google may process your voice under their own policies; on Android the app asks for on-device recognition first.
Web pages and linksWhen you add a web page or open a link, the app loads that site as a browser would, and the site sees your connection as it would in a browser. It may set cookies in the app's web view, which you can clear on Windows.
Scanning a link code (Android)Uses Google's code scanner in Google Play services. The app doesn't need camera permission and receives only the code.
Sharing and exportingWhen you export a PDF or share a file, a readable copy goes where you choose to send it. Temporary copies made for sharing are kept in the app's private storage and cleared.

The app doesn't include AI features that send your content to an AI service. If we add any, they'll only run when you start them on a page or notebook, and this policy will say which service receives what before they're released.

Permissions

  • Android: internet access (for sync, web pages and the handwriting model) and biometric access (to unlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN). The app doesn't ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • Windows: the app runs in its own package and stores its files in its own app data folder.

Problem logs

If the app closes unexpectedly, it writes a short problem log on your device, with the app version, the device type and where the error happened. It never contains your notes. It stays on your device unless you choose to share it with us.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to run and secure the service, under Netlify's privacy policy.

Removing your data

  • On a device: delete notebooks in the app, or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the app's files from that device.
  • Google Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, find MyPowerfulNotes (or NotePen, its earlier name) and choose Delete hidden app data. To remove its access, visit your Google Account's third-party access page.
  • OneDrive: delete the app's folder under Apps in your OneDrive, and remove its access at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Backup files you've saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

There's no account with us to delete, because we don't hold any of your data.

Children

The app isn't designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal information about you, there's normally nothing for us to give you access to or correct. If you have a question or a complaint about privacy, contact us and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we'll update this policy and the date at the top before the change is released. For significant changes, the app will also tell you.
