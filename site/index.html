One page, everything on it

Each page is a sheet of paper you write on with a pen. Everything else sits on the same page, so your notes stay together the way they would in a paper notebook.

  • Ink that feels like a pen

    Pen, fineliner and highlighter with pressure, in your own colours. Low-latency ink on both Surface and S Pen.

  • Typed text and tables

    Headings, bullet lists and tables that wrap the same on every device.

  • Drawings to scale

    Lengths, angles, areas and sums. Change one length and the joined lines follow, keeping corners square.

  • PDFs to write on

    Import a PDF or save a web page, then highlight, circle and write in the margins.

  • Ticks and flags become actions

    Draw a tick box or flag a line, and it lands in one Actions list with a due date and owner.

  • Links and sticky notes

    Add web links and keep them as resources for the page, section or notebook.

Paper for every job

Choose ruled, grid, dot, Cornell, planner, music or isometric paper, or design your own. Pages come in A5 to A0, portrait or landscape, or as one continuous page that keeps going.

  • Ruled
  • Grid
  • Dots
  • Cornell
  • Day planner
  • Music
  • Isometric
  • Your own

  • Search everything

    Typed text, handwriting and PDF text, across every notebook.

  • A map of your notes

    See notebooks, pages and tags as a map, and add your own thoughts and links.

  • Export when you need to

    Send a page or notebook as a PDF, with links that still work.

  • Photos and pictures

    Take a photo with your tablet or phone, or add a picture, and write on and around it.

  • Fill

    Tap inside a shape you've drawn to colour it in, under the ink.

  • Talk to text

    Dictate into a text box using your device's own speech recognition.

  • Sections and headers

    Split notebooks into sections and add a header and footer to every page.

  • Daily snapshots

    A snapshot of your notes each day, and backup files that stay encrypted.

Private by design

MyPowerfulNotes has no server and no account with us. Your notes are encrypted on your device before they're saved, and they sync through your own cloud storage as encrypted files.

Read the privacy policy

Encrypted on your device
Every page, PDF and setting is stored with AES-256-GCM encryption, using a key that stays on your device.
Only you hold the recovery code
Your recovery code opens your notes on a new device. Nobody else has it, so nobody else can open them.
Your cloud, your files
Sync uses your own OneDrive or Google Drive, which only ever sees encrypted files.
No tracking
No ads, no analytics and no crash reports sent anywhere.
Nothing leaves unless you choose
Sync, opening web pages and sharing a file only happen when you start them.
Backups stay encrypted
Backup files are as protected as your notes. Export a PDF when you want a copy anyone can read.

Windows and Android, the same notebooks

Both apps read and write the same encrypted files, so you can start a page on your PC and finish it on your tablet.

Windows

For Windows 11 PCs and tablets with a pen, such as Surface.

  • Pen, mouse, touch and keyboard
  • Reads your handwriting so you can search it
  • Shows a code to link your tablet or phone

Android

For tablets and phones running Android 11 or later, including S Pen devices.

  • Tablet and phone layouts
  • Unlock with your fingerprint, face or PIN
  • Scan the code on your PC to set up in a minute
