Get help or send feedback

To report a problem, ask a question or suggest a feature, open an issue on GitHub. Say which device you're using and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Issues are public. Never post your recovery code, and don't include anything from your notes or personal details. If you need to talk privately, say so in the issue and we'll arrange a private way to reply.

Common questions

I've lost my recovery code

If you can still open your notes on your PC, make a new one: open More, then App lock, and choose New recovery code. The old code stops working, so store the new one somewhere safe, away from your devices. If you can't unlock your notes on any device and don't have the code, they can't be recovered: the code is the only key, and nobody else holds it.

How do I move my notes to a new device?

Turn on sync on your current device (More, then Sync), then install the app on the new one and choose Set up from OneDrive or Set up from Google Drive. On a PC you can choose Link a phone or tablet and scan the code with the new device instead of typing your recovery code. You can also open a backup file on the new device and unlock it with your recovery code.

How do backups work?

The app takes a snapshot of your notes once a day and keeps the last 14. You can also save backup files, or have one saved every day to a folder you choose (the newest 3 are kept). Backup files stay encrypted and open with your recovery code.

Can I use the app without sync?

Yes. Sync is optional, and everything works offline. Without sync, keep regular backup files, because your notes are only on that device.

Why does a page say it was saved by a newer version?

Another of your devices has a newer version of the app that saved something this one doesn't understand yet. Update the app on this device. Until then, it won't change those notes, so nothing is lost.

The app closed unexpectedly

The app keeps a short problem log on your device, which never contains your notes. On Android it offers to share the log when it next starts. Attach it to an issue on GitHub with a description of what you were doing.

Which devices does it work on?

Windows 11 PCs and tablets (a pen is recommended), and Android 11 or later on tablets and phones, including Samsung S Pen devices.

NotePen? Is that the same app?

Yes. MyPowerfulNotes was called NotePen while it was first being built. Existing notes, sync and backups carry on as they were.

Removing your data

MyPowerfulNotes doesn't have accounts, so there's nothing to delete with us. To remove your notes:

  • From a device: uninstall the app (or delete notebooks in it).
  • From Google Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, find MyPowerfulNotes (or NotePen) and choose Delete hidden app data. Remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • From OneDrive: delete the app's folder under Apps, and remove its access at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Backup files are wherever you saved them; delete them there.
